# Moon surface maps

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio.

Source: [CGI Moon Kit](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/), based on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera (LROC) and laser altimeter (LOLA) data.

- `lroc-color.jpg`: the 2025 2048 × 1024 color map, downloaded from https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
- `lola-height.jpg`: the 1024 × 512 elevation preview, downloaded from https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_3_8bit.jpg

Maps are served locally. Color supplies lunar maria and crater detail; elevation supplies restrained surface relief.
