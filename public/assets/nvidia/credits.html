3D asset credits

Selected materials and the wheelie-bin model are adapted from NVIDIA’s SimReady assets, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

  • Concrete: environment_floor_a01 — resized to 512 pixels, with softer color and contrast.
  • Brushed aluminium: barrier_wall_plastic_orange_a03 — resized to 256 pixels, with a muted painted finish.
  • Wheelie bin: trashCan_wheeled_green_a01 — simplified geometry, green and charcoal materials, adjusted scale, and converted from USD to glTF. Simulation components and original textures are omitted.

These adaptations were made for this miniature city. NVIDIA does not endorse this project.

Moon imagery

The moon uses color and elevation maps from NASA’s CGI Moon Kit, based on LRO camera and laser-altimeter data. Credit: NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio. The maps are served locally and shaded as a three-dimensional sphere.
