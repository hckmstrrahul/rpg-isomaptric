Assets and tools

  • NVIDIA SimReady: adapted concrete and metal maps, and a simplified wheelie-bin model. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Sources, adaptation details and license.
  • NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio: CGI Moon Kit, based on Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera and laser altimeter data. Local processing notes.
  • Inter: Rasmus Andersson and contributors, SIL Open Font License 1.1.
  • Aref Ruqaa: The Aref Ruqaa Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for the museum’s procedural calligraphic glazing. The short inscription excerpts are by H.H. Sheikh Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum; the actual building’s calligraphy is by Mattar bin Lahej. Museum reference.
  • Hugeicons free icons: MIT licensed. License.
  • Three.js, React, Sonner and polygon-clipping: their package licenses apply. Dependency versions are recorded in the lockfile.

Map composition inspiration. These are artistically scaled city and district miniatures, not navigation maps or affiliated representations of the attractions. Moon Base is an invented lunar settlement. New landmarks and lunar hardware are original procedural models; official photographs and maps were used as architectural and geographic references.
