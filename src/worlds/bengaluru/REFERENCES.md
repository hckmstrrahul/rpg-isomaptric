# Bengaluru — civic district and Cubbon Park

This chapter is a compressed, north-up artistic miniature of central Bengaluru, approximately 12.9715–12.9830° N, 77.5862–77.5983° E. It preserves the named landmarks' relative district relationships, while simplifying road bends, park paths and unnamed perimeter blocks for play. It is not a surveyed or navigational map. Buildings, trees, paving and vehicle details are original procedural geometry. No reference photograph or map tile is shipped as a texture.

## Sources used for form and position

- [Karnataka Legislature: Vidhana Soudha](https://kla.kar.nic.in/council/vds.htm) — broad 700 × 350 ft granite footprint, 150 ft crown, central dome and six smaller domes, twelve entrance columns, and Neo-Dravidian details. The model emphasizes the long wings and two open courts, cornices and stair-front colonnade.
- [Bengaluru Urban district tourism](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/tourism/) — Vidhana Soudha, adjacent Vikasa Soudha, red Graeco-Roman Attara Kacheri and central institutions.
- [Bengaluru Urban: Cubbon Park](https://bengaluruurban.nic.in/en/tourist-place/cubbon-park/) — Sri Chamarajendra Park, the civic district setting, Vidhana Soudha and High Court at its edge.
- [Supreme Court e-Committee: Karnataka High Court history](https://ecommitteesci.gov.in/project/history-hck-bengaluru/) — Attara Kacheri opposite Vidhana Soudha.
- [KSTDC Bengaluru pocket guide](https://kstdc.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Bengaluru_Pocket_Hi_Res_compressed.pdf) — inspected its page 6 aerial photograph for the red court's two courtyards and gabled roofs, the broad legislature behind it, and the dense tree setting; central-city landmark descriptions informed the crop.
- [Moheen Reeyad’s close photograph of the Vidhana Soudha dome](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dome_with_Lion_Capital_of_Ashoka,_Vidhana_Soudha_(01).jpg) — inspected for the octagonal pavilion, corbelled balcony, masonry coursing, bulbous dome and cardinal chaithya ornaments; photo is referenced, not embedded or used as a texture.
- [Incredible India: Bengaluru](https://www.incredibleindia.gov.in/en/karnataka/bengaluru) — Vidhana Soudha northwest of Cubbon Park and Government Museum on Kasturba Road.

## Deliberate crop and fidelity limits

The image supplied by the user establishes isometric miniature depth and lush vegetation, not the inland city's geography. There is no invented ocean, cathedral or island. Lalbagh, Bangalore Palace and outer technology districts fall outside this civic crop. The six architectural models represent Vidhana Soudha, Attara Kacheri, Vikasa Soudha, Seshadri Iyer Memorial Hall, Government Museum and the UB City group. Unnamed perimeter blocks are local urban fabric rather than invented named monuments. Park paths are simplified connected walks, not surveyed path geometry. Night lighting is an artistic restrained treatment, not a claim about a site's opening hours or illumination schedule.

## Performance

All building solids are merged by material per destructible root. Window inserts are merged geometry, never individual window meshes or lights. Two instanced tree species and one shrub batch form the canopy. Mature rain-tree groves use three shared scale bands; their crowns shade footpaths while trunks and road crowns remain clear. Five bounded street-building families vary massing, roofs, glazing and balconies. The original procedural granite texture supplies restrained stone joints, and the cut soil section extends to −18. Lamp emitters share the existing 12-light pool; all ground washes share one batch. The dormant water mesh is hidden and the plan disables reflection water. The chapter imports no other chapter's plan, architecture or geometry.
